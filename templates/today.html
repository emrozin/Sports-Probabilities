How to read this

"Model" is the model's chance for its favorite (the pick shown); "Market" is the betting market's chance for the same result, with the bookmaker's margin taken out (Premier League: the average bookmaker; NFL: nflverse's moneyline; NBA and college: DraftKings via ESPN). After a game, the pick turns green or red. "Biggest disagreements" are the games where model and market differ most; on past seasons the market has usually been the one that's right, so read them as "the model sees it differently", not as tips.
