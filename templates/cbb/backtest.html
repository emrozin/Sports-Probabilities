Every week since 2010–11, the model was trained only on earlier games, then asked to predict that week. Settings were tuned on 2010–11 to 2017–18; {{ from }} to {{ to }} is the honest test.

Why Elo?

There are no free historical betting odds for college basketball, so the model is compared with a simple Elo rating: one number per team, moved after every game by how surprising the result was (more for bigger wins), and pulled a quarter of the way back to average each new season. Its settings are common ones, not tuned here.
