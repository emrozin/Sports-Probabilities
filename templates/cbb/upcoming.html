Upcoming games

The model's chance of each team winning, for every Division I game in the next eight days.

How to read this

The big numbers are the model's chance of each team winning; the bar shows the same split, and the table the expected margin and total points. Where ESPN shows a betting line (DraftKings), the thin bar and the second row give the market's view, with the bookmaker's margin taken out. There are no free historical lines for college basketball, so the backtest compares the model with a simple Elo rating instead. The conference shown next to each team is its conference this season.
