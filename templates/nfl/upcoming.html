How to read this

The big numbers are the model's chance of each team winning. The thick bar shows the same split; the thin bar under it is the betting market's view, from the moneylines with the bookmaker's margin removed. The table compares the model's expected margin and total points with the market's spread and total.

A tag like Model 7 pts higher on DEN marks a game where the model gives a team a chance of winning at least 5 points higher than the market does. Each team is shown in its own colors; when two are too alike, the away team wears its second color.
