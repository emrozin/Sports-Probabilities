Upcoming games

The model's chance of each team winning, from who's playing: every player's rating and expected minutes, with injured players left out.

How to read this

The big numbers are the model's chance of each team winning; the thick bar shows the same split, and the thin one the betting market's (DraftKings, via ESPN, with the bookmaker's margin taken out) when there's a line. The table gives the expected winning margin and total points next to the market's spread and total. "Out" lists players ESPN's injury report rules out (Out or Doubtful); their minutes go to their teammates. "Model 6 pts higher" marks games where the model and the market disagree by 5 percentage points or more.
