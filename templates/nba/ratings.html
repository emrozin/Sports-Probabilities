How to read this

A player's rating is how many points per game his team gains with him on the floor for a whole game, against an average player, after allowing for everyone else on both teams, from the last three seasons with recent games counting most. A team's rating adds up its players' ratings weighted by their expected minutes, plus a smaller team term (coaching, system, fit). "Healthy" uses everyone's usual minutes; "Now" leaves out the players the injury report rules out. Home court is worth {{ home_adv }} points.

"Impact" is a player's rating times his share of a game on the floor (his usual minutes out of 48): what he's worth to his team per game. Ratings for players with few minutes are pulled toward average, so they're less certain.
