How this works

Each player's points, rebounds, assists and threes are projected from his recent rate per minute and his expected minutes, as a share of what the model expects his team to get, so when a teammate is out his share goes up. The chance of reaching a line comes from how widely results have spread around projections in past seasons. The lines considered: {% for k, v in lines.items() %}{{ k }} {{ v }}{% if not loop.last %}; {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Easy ones (like 10+ points for a star) are left out: only points from 15, rebounds from 8, assists from 5 and threes from 2.

Likely to hit isn't the same as a good bet: the sportsbook's price matters, and these aren't compared with it. On 2017–18 to 2025–26 the projections missed by 2–3% less than each player's season average for points, assists and threes (rebounds about the same), and the chances were honest: props given 60–75% hit 67% of the time, 75–90% 81%, and the five likeliest each day hit 92% (predicted 94%).
