How this works

Every remaining game gets a random result from the model: the expected margin from each team's players and their expected minutes (players on the injury report are left out until their expected return date), plus the usual game-to-game spread. Each simulated season also shifts every team's strength by a random amount (typically about {{ rating_sd }} points per game), because ratings are estimates and rosters change through the season.

Standings are worked out with head-to-head record and conference record for ties. Then the play-in (7th v 8th for the 7th seed; the loser plays the winner of 9th v 10th for the 8th) and four best-of-seven rounds, the better seed at home in games 1, 2, 5 and 7. "Top 6" means straight into the playoffs; "Playoffs" includes getting there through the play-in. A dash means it didn't happen in any simulation; that's not the same as being eliminated.
