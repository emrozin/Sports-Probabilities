How the simulation works

Each game still to play is played out from the model's expected goals, with the results so far kept. Ties on points are split by goal difference, goals scored, away goals, wins and away wins (UEFA's later tie-breakers aren't modelled). The knockout bracket follows the seeding rules: 9th and 10th meet 23rd or 24th, and so on, the winners meet the top eight, and 1st and 2nd are kept in opposite halves. Two-legged ties include extra time and penalties; the final is on a neutral ground. Once the real pairings of a round are drawn, they're used.
