How the simulation works

Each game still to play is played out from the model's expected goals (with the home advantage of league games), and the results so far are kept. Ties on points are split by goal difference, then goals scored, then at random (head-to-head records aren't modelled). "Top 4" is a top-four finish, the usual Champions League places; "Bottom 3" is the bottom three, the relegation places (in leagues with a play-off, 3rd from bottom goes to the play-off).
