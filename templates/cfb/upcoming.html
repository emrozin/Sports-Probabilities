Upcoming games

The model's chance of each team winning, for every game involving a top-division (FBS) school in the next eight days.

How to read this

The big numbers are the model's chance of each team winning; the bar shows the same split. The free data has no betting odds for the current season yet, so upcoming games are compared with CollegeFootballData's Elo rating instead. Finished games are compared with the betting market's closing odds where available (2006–2025). The conference shown next to each team is its conference this season.
